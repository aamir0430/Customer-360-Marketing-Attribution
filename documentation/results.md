# Project Results
## Customer 360 & Marketing Attribution Programme

## 1. Overview

This document records implementation validation results and, in later phases, analytical findings and business insights generated by the Customer 360 & Marketing Attribution platform.

## 2. Phase 1 – Data Source Setup and Dataset Generation

**Status: Completed.**

Three synthetic datasets were generated:
- `synthetic_crm_customers.csv`
- `synthetic_ad_spend.csv`
- `identity_mapping.csv`

The GA4 public ecommerce sample dataset was selected as the website behavioural data source.

## 3. Phase 2 – Identity Resolution

**Status: Completed.**

The final curated BigQuery table was created:

`customer360-mta.customer_360_curated.identity_resolved_sessions`

It combines GA4 session data with synthetic identity mapping and CRM customer attributes where a mapping is available. LEFT JOIN logic retains sessions without a match.

### 3.1 Final Validation Results

| Measure | Result |
|---|---:|
| Total sessions in output | 360,129 |
| Unique sessions | 360,129 |
| Identified sessions | 10,246 |
| Unidentified sessions | 349,883 |
| CRM customers represented among identified sessions | 3,500 |
| Missing CRM customer records in identified output | 0 |
| Mismatched synthetic IDs in validation | 0 |

The identified and unidentified session counts reconcile:

10,246 + 349,883 = 360,129.

### 3.2 Interpretation

The final table preserves the complete GA4 session population. A subset of sessions is linked to CRM customer records through the generated identity mapping dataset; remaining sessions are retained with an `UNIDENTIFIED` status.

The zero missing CRM records and zero mismatched synthetic IDs indicate that the final output passed the recorded referential consistency checks against the synthetic source data.

### 3.3 Limitation

The identity mappings are synthetic. These results demonstrate the operation and internal consistency of the identity-resolution workflow against generated test data. They are not a measure of real-world identity resolution accuracy.
