# Business and Technical Definitions

## Customer Analytics

**Customer 360:** A consolidated analytical view of customer information and behaviour across available data sources.

**Identity Resolution:** The process of connecting records that refer to the same customer across different systems.

**Identified Customer:** A website-side record successfully associated with a CRM customer identifier.

**Unidentified Customer Activity:** Website activity for which no valid CRM mapping exists.

**RFM Analysis:** Customer analysis based on Recency, Frequency and Monetary Value.

**Recency:** How recently a customer purchased.

**Frequency:** How often a customer purchased during the analysis period.

**Monetary Value:** The monetary value of customer purchases during the analysis period.

**Customer Lifetime Value (CLV):** An analytical estimate of the value generated by a customer over a defined lifetime or modelling horizon.

## Data Engineering

**BigQuery:** Google's cloud data warehouse.

**dbt Core:** A SQL-based transformation framework used to build, test and document analytical data models.

**Staging Layer:** The layer that standardizes and prepares source data.

**Intermediate Layer:** The layer containing reusable transformation logic.

**Marts Layer:** The layer containing business-ready analytical models.

**Fact Table:** A table containing measurable business events or transactions.

**Dimension Table:** A table containing descriptive attributes used for analysis.

## Marketing Attribution

**Marketing Attribution:** The allocation of conversion credit across marketing touchpoints.

**First-Touch Attribution:** Assigns conversion credit to the first eligible touchpoint.

**Last-Touch Attribution:** Assigns conversion credit to the final eligible touchpoint before conversion.

**Linear Attribution:** Distributes conversion credit equally across eligible touchpoints.

**Time-Decay Attribution:** Assigns relatively greater credit to touchpoints closer to conversion.

**Touchpoint:** An interaction between a customer and a marketing channel.

**Conversion:** A defined desired action, such as a purchase.

## Marketing Performance

**Impressions:** Number of times an advertisement is displayed.

**Clicks:** Number of times users click an advertisement.

**CTR:** Click-through rate, calculated as clicks divided by impressions.

**CPC:** Cost per click, calculated as advertising expenditure divided by clicks.

**CPA:** Cost per acquisition, calculated as advertising expenditure divided by conversions.

**ROAS:** Return on advertising spend, generally calculated as attributed revenue divided by advertising expenditure.

**ROI:** Return on investment. Its interpretation depends on whether the calculation uses revenue, profit or incremental financial impact.

**Campaign Mapping Coverage:** The extent to which campaign identifiers or source/medium combinations can be reliably connected to analytical traffic and conversion data.

## Data Quality

**Data Validation:** Checking whether data satisfies defined structural and business rules.

**Reconciliation:** Comparing related financial or analytical totals to identify discrepancies.

**Synthetic Data:** Artificially generated data used for development, testing or demonstration.

**Simulated Conversion:** A conversion value generated for analytical demonstration rather than verified through a live production advertising platform.
